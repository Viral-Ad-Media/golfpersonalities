Phil Mickelson, known to fans as "Lefty", is one of the most successful and most talked-about golfers of the modern era. Fans know him for his short game, his attacking style and his willingness to try shots other players wouldn't. Few careers have been as long or as eventful.

Early life

Mickelson was born in San Diego, California, in 1970. He is right-handed in everyday life but plays golf left-handed. The often-told reason is that he learned by mirroring his father's right-handed swing. He was a star amateur and won the PGA Tour's Northern Telecom Open in 1991 while still a student at Arizona State University. Very few amateurs have won on tour.

A career built on majors

Mickelson won six major championships:

  • Masters: 2004, 2006 and 2010
  • PGA Championship: 2005 and 2021
  • The Open Championship: 2013, at Muirfield

His 2021 PGA Championship win at Kiawah Island made him, at 50, the oldest major champion in history. The U.S. Open is the one major he never won. He finished runner-up a record six times, which is why he never completed the career Grand Slam.

Across his PGA Tour career he won more than 40 events and spent years ranked inside the world's top 10. He also played on many U.S. Ryder Cup and Presidents Cup teams.

Family

Phil married Amy McBride in 1996. They have three children: Amanda, Sophia and Evan. In 2009 Amy and Phil's mother were both diagnosed with breast cancer, and Phil stepped away from the tour for a time to support them. The family has been involved in charity work through the Phil and Amy Mickelson Foundation.

LIV Golf

In 2022 Mickelson became one of the first high-profile players to join the Saudi-backed LIV Golf league. The move, and comments he made beforehand, caused a huge controversy. He took several months away from the game before he joined LIV, and he has since played on the league while still entering the majors he is eligible for.

Career earnings and net worth

Mickelson's official PGA Tour prize money is among the highest in the history of the tour, at more than $90 million. Over his career, endorsements with major brands have earned him far more than prize money, and his LIV Golf contract was widely reported to be worth a very large sum.

Published estimates of his net worth vary a lot and often put it in the hundreds of millions of dollars. However, none of these figures is officially confirmed, so treat any single number with caution.

Legacy

Whatever people think of his recent choices, Mickelson's place in golf history is secure. He won six majors over three decades, played with a creativity that made him a fan favourite, and set a record by winning a major at 50.
