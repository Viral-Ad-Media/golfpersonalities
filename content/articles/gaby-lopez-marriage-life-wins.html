Gaby Lopez has become one of the leading figures in Mexican sport. She is a multiple LPGA Tour winner and has proudly represented Mexico on the world stage. She followed in the footsteps of Lorena Ochoa, Mexico's greatest golfer, and built a career of her own.

Early life

María Gabriela López was born in Mexico City in 1993. She took up golf as a child and quickly became one of the country's top junior players.

College golf at Arkansas

Lopez moved to the United States to play college golf at the University of Arkansas. She became an All-American and one of the program's standout players, and the move helped prepare her for professional golf in the U.S.

Turning professional

She turned pro in 2015 and earned her LPGA Tour card. Her early seasons were a learning curve, but she gradually established herself among the tour's regular contenders.

LPGA Tour wins

  • 2018 Blue Bay LPGA: her first LPGA win came on Hainan Island in China. She became the first Mexican winner on the LPGA Tour since Lorena Ochoa.
  • 2020 Diamond Resorts Tournament of Champions: she won a marathon seven-hole playoff against Nasa Hataoka and Inbee Park in Florida. It was one of the longest playoffs in LPGA history.

Olympian and flag bearer

Lopez represented Mexico in the women's golf event at the Rio 2016 Olympics and again at Tokyo 2020. In Tokyo she was one of Mexico's flag bearers at the opening ceremony, a great honour for a golfer in a country with a rich sporting tradition.

Life away from the course

Lopez keeps much of her personal life private. Publicly, she focuses on her career, her family's support and her pride in representing Mexico. She is known for her positive attitude and her connection with fans, especially Mexican and Latin American supporters who follow her at events around the world.

Why she matters

When Lorena Ochoa retired in 2010, Mexico lost its greatest golfer. Gaby Lopez helped fill that space. Her wins and Olympic appearances have kept Mexican golf on the world stage and inspired a new generation of players at home.
