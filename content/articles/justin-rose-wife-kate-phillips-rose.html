Justin Rose is one of the most consistent English golfers of his generation. He won the U.S. Open, won Olympic gold and reached world No. 1. Through most of that career, his wife Kate Phillips Rose has been in his corner, and fans often see her walking the fairways at the biggest events.

Who is Kate Phillips Rose?

Kate Phillips grew up in England and competed in gymnastics before the couple's life became tied to the professional tour. She and Justin met when they were young, long before he was a major champion. They married in 2006.

Kate keeps a much lower profile than many tour spouses. Over the years she has stayed involved behind the scenes: she helped manage the family's busy travel schedule and took a leading role in the couple's charity work.

Family life

The Roses have two children, a son, Leo, and a daughter, Charlotte. Bringing up a young family while following a global tour schedule shaped many of their choices, including where they based themselves. For much of Justin's career the family lived in the Bahamas and spent time in Florida, which suited a player competing mainly on the PGA Tour.

The Kate & Justin Rose Foundation

The couple set up the Kate & Justin Rose Foundation (often shortened to the KJR Foundation) to support children. Its focus has been on education, wellbeing and opportunity for children who need extra help. Kate has been closely involved in its work, which is one reason she is often described as a partner in Justin's career rather than a spectator.

Justin's biggest moments

Kate was there for the high points that defined Justin's career:

  • 1998 Open Championship: as a 17-year-old amateur he finished tied fourth at Royal Birkdale, holing a famous pitch on the final hole.
  • 2013 U.S. Open: he won his first major at Merion, the first English winner of the U.S. Open since Tony Jacklin in 1970.
  • 2016 Olympics: he won the gold medal when golf returned to the Games in Rio de Janeiro.
  • 2018: he rose to world No. 1 and won the FedEx Cup.
  • Ryder Cup: he has been a long-time fixture of European teams.

Why their partnership stands out

Life on tour is hard on relationships. There are weeks away from home, changing time zones and the pressure of results. The Roses have managed to build a family and a charitable foundation alongside a long, successful career, and that is a big part of why fans are curious about the woman behind one of England's best golfers.
