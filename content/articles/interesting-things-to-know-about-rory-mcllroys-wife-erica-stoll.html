Rory McIlroy is one of the most recognisable golfers in the world. His wife, Erica Stoll, is far less public. She seldom gives interviews and keeps her social media private. Still, her story is woven into some of the best-known moments of Rory's career.

1. She worked in golf before she married into it

Erica grew up in Rochester, New York. Before she was known as Rory's wife, she worked for the PGA of America and helped run championships, including the Ryder Cup. That gave her an insider's view of how professional golf works that few tour spouses have.

2. She helped save Rory's Ryder Cup in 2012

The story fans tell most often comes from the 2012 Ryder Cup at Medinah. On the Sunday, Rory mixed up time zones and almost missed his singles tee time. A police escort rushed him to the course, and Erica, who was working for the PGA of America that week, is widely credited with helping to raise the alarm and get him there in time. He made it with minutes to spare and won his match, and Europe completed the "Miracle at Medinah".

3. Their relationship became public in 2015

The pair had known each other for several years before they were seen together publicly in 2015. They got engaged later that year.

4. They married in Ireland in 2017

Rory and Erica married in April 2017 at Ashford Castle in County Mayo, Ireland. The wedding was private, and the couple shared very few details or photos, which fits Erica's preference for privacy.

5. They have a daughter, Poppy

Their daughter, Poppy Kennedy McIlroy, was born in 2020. Rory has spoken often about how fatherhood changed his perspective on golf and on life away from the course.

6. Their marriage made headlines in 2024, and they reconciled

In May 2024 Rory filed for divorce, and the news made headlines around the world. A few weeks later he withdrew the filing and said the couple had resolved their differences and looked forward to a future together. Both have kept the details private.

7. She was there for the career Grand Slam

In April 2025 Rory won the Masters at Augusta National. It was his fifth major and completed the career Grand Slam. Erica and Poppy were among the first people he celebrated with after a long chase for the green jacket.

8. She stays out of the spotlight by choice

Many golf partners build public profiles of their own. Erica has done the opposite: she seldom posts publicly and avoids media attention. For a player as famous as Rory, that privacy gives the family a rare bit of normal life.
