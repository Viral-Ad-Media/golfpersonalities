Golfers can be surprisingly tricky to buy for. They often have strong opinions about their equipment. The good news is that plenty of gifts every golfer will appreciate don't require you to know their swing speed. Here are ideas for every budget.

Small gifts and stocking fillers

  • Golf balls: every golfer uses them. Find out their preferred ball, or order a personalised dozen.
  • A personalised ball marker or divot tool: small, useful and easy to engrave.
  • Tees and a ball-marking stencil: practical and inexpensive.
  • A quality golf towel: often overlooked and always needed.
  • Golf gloves: they wear out quickly, so spares are welcome (check the size and hand).

Mid-range gifts

  • A putting mat: for practice at home or in the office.
  • Headcovers: a fun or classic set of headcovers adds personality to the bag.
  • Golf clothing: a good waterproof jacket, polo shirts or a cap from their favourite brand or club.
  • A golf book: biographies of great players and classic instruction books make thoughtful gifts.
  • A practice net: great if they have space in a garden or garage.

Bigger gifts

  • A rangefinder or GPS watch: one of the most useful upgrades for any regular golfer.
  • A push cart or electric trolley: perfect for golfers who walk.
  • A personal launch monitor: ideal for data-driven golfers.
  • A new golf bag: a fresh stand or cart bag is always appreciated.

Experiences

  • Lessons with a coach: one of the best gifts for any golfer who wants to improve.
  • A round at a bucket-list course: a green fee voucher for a famous or highly rated course nearby.
  • A custom club fitting: helps them find equipment that suits their swing.
  • Tickets to a professional event: watching the best players in person is unforgettable.

Gift-buying tips

If you're unsure about equipment, ask a playing partner or the golfer's local pro shop. A gift card to their favourite golf retailer or club is always a safe bet.
