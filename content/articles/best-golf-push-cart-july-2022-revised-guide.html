Walking is one of golf's great pleasures, but carrying a full bag for 18 holes takes its toll. A push cart, also called a trolley, takes the weight off your shoulders. It also keeps your bag stable and gives you room for drinks, a scorecard and your phone.

Three wheels or two?

Three-wheel carts are the most popular choice. They're stable, they're easy to push with one hand and they stay upright when you park them. Two-wheel carts are lighter and usually cheaper, but you pull them behind you and they can be less stable on slopes.

What to look for

  • Folded size: measure your car boot. Some carts fold almost flat, others stay bulky.
  • Weight: lighter carts are easier to lift in and out of the car. Most good models weigh between 5 and 10 kg.
  • Ease of folding: a one-step fold saves time in the car park.
  • Foot brake: essential on hilly courses, so the cart doesn't roll away while you play.
  • Wheels: bigger wheels roll better over rough ground, and quick-release wheels make storage easier.
  • Bag fit: check that the cart's straps and base work with your bag, especially if you use a cart bag with a non-slip base.
  • Accessories: scorecard holder, umbrella mount, cup holder and storage console.

Popular brands to compare

  • Clicgear: known for sturdy three-wheel carts with a compact fold.
  • Bag Boy: a long-established brand with a wide range of three-wheel models at different prices.
  • Sun Mountain: a golf bag maker whose carts are popular for their light weight and quick fold.
  • Big Max: a European favourite with very compact folding designs.

Look at the current models from each brand, since features and prices change from season to season.

Push cart or electric trolley?

Electric trolleys do the pushing for you, and some models follow you around the course. They cost more and need charging, but they're a great option for golfers with back or joint problems or anyone who plays hilly courses often.

Our advice

For most golfers, a mid-priced three-wheel cart with a foot brake and a compact fold is the sweet spot. Check it fits your car, try the fold in the shop if you can, and you'll enjoy walking the course for years.
