When Michelle Wie got married in 2019 she became Michelle Wie West, and she married into one of the best-known families in basketball. Her husband, Jonnie West, has built his own career in the NBA.

Who is Jonnie West?

Jonnie West is the son of Jerry West, the Hall of Fame player and executive whose silhouette is widely said to have inspired the NBA logo. Jonnie played college basketball at West Virginia University, his father's alma mater. He then went into the business side of the game and joined the front office of the Golden State Warriors. There he worked in basketball operations and rose to a senior role during the team's championship era.

How they met

Their paths crossed in the San Francisco Bay Area, where Wie had studied at Stanford and West was working for the Warriors. Their relationship became public in 2018, and they announced their engagement that year.

The wedding

The couple married in August 2019 in Beverly Hills, surrounded by family and friends from the golf and basketball worlds. Afterwards Michelle began competing as Michelle Wie West.

Starting a family

Their daughter, Makenna Kamalei Yoona West, was born in June 2020. Her middle names honour her Hawaiian and Korean heritage. Michelle has shared parts of her life as a mother on social media and has spoken about how motherhood changed her priorities.

Supporting each other's careers

Being married to a professional athlete means a lot of time apart. Michelle travelled for LPGA events and Jonnie worked through a long NBA season. Michelle has spoken publicly about how much Jonnie's support meant when she returned to competition in 2021 after giving birth, and later when she stepped back from full-time golf.

The couple has also faced loss together. Jerry West died in June 2024, and tributes poured in from across the sports world.

A two-sport family

Together, Michelle and Jonnie bring together two very different sporting worlds, and they've built a life around both: courtside at Warriors games and greenside at golf's biggest events.
